Absence isn’t cancellation.
An export may cover a different date range or filter. “Removed” only means a record was absent from the second file. A cancellation needs an explicit STATUS or METHOD signal.
01 / TIME

Masters, explicit exceptions and rule changes are compared. Recurring instances are never expanded or guessed.
